# 🤝 Contributing to AlignUp

Thank you for considering contributing to **AlignUp** — a project built on clarity, purpose, and human-centered productivity.  
Your ideas, suggestions, and pull requests are always welcome. 🌿

---

## 🧭 Our Philosophy
We believe productivity should feel peaceful.  
Every contribution should move AlignUp closer to its mission:  
> To help people do the right things, at the right time, with the right energy.

---

## 🛠 Ways to Contribute
- 🧩 **Improve the codebase:** Optimize existing Python modules or refactor logic.  
- 🎨 **Enhance the UI:** Suggest or design simple, accessible interface improvements.  
- 🧠 **Add features:** Implement new ideas that fit the vision (e.g., reflection analytics, AI nudges).  
- 📚 **Improve documentation:** Fix typos, clarify setup steps, or expand feature explanations.  
- 💬 **Share feedback:** Open an issue to discuss potential improvements.

---
